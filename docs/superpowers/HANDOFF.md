## Container notes

Gradle downloads hit Maven Central 429s for minutes at a time: loop `./gradlew ... --max-workers=2 -q` with a 40 s sleep until the log has no "429" or "Could not resolve". Against a 2024-2025 `accuracy.db` (no 2026), two `OpportunitiesRepositoryTest` cases and `ProjectionsContractTest > every team's projected week adds up to one game` fail on missing 2026 data; use a 2024-2026 `stats.db` for them.
